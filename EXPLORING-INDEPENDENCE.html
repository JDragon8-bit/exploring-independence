The Declaration of Independence was a letter sent to the current King of England, King George III. It was written primarily by Thomas Jefferson with help from John Adams, Benjamin Franklin, Roger Sherman and Robert R. Livingston. It was written between June 11 and 28, 1776. Congress approved the final draft on July 4, 1776. The preamble is the introduction to the Declaration. It outlines "inalienable rights" which are rights that people are born with and cannot be taken away. It then lists 27 complaints or grievances that the colonists had with King George. It finally ends by saying that the colonies are free to make their own decisions and are independent.
